# Known Limitations

- Android/device/operator restrictions may prevent some cellular metrics or neighbor cells from appearing.
- Cell updates can be throttled by Android; invalid/unreported values must display N/A.
- Online maps require a provider and connectivity. Offline grid mode does not include third-party tile downloads.
- Indoor pins are floorplan-relative, not a substitute for GPS coordinates.
- Candidate Lite session and site-preview limits require verification in the release binary.
- The external speed-test widget consumes network data and depends on its provider's service.
- RFeX Drive Lite offers Android-supported read-only RF observation; capabilities outside this public edition are not advertised here.
