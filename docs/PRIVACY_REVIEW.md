# Privacy information for the Lite review build

Lite reads the location and cellular details Android exposes when you grant the applicable permissions. Saved sessions stay in app storage until exported through Android. You can delete local sessions in Saved Sessions or change access in Android Settings.

Enabling online maps sends your IP address and requested tile areas to OpenStreetMap. Opening OpenSpeedTest Public sends your IP address to the provider, and starting its test sends measurement traffic. Results stay in the provider widget. Cloudflare hosted tests are disabled in the release build. Google Mobile Ads and consent SDKs are included. Production ad handling and related declarations require final verification.

A final privacy policy, verified contact and Play Data safety declaration are pending.
