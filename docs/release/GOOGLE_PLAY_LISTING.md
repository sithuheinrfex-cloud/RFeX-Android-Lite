## 2. Full Description (Max 4,000 characters)

```text
RFeX Drive Lite is an on-device cellular radio frequency (RF) engineering tool designed for network optimization engineers, field drive-test technicians, wireless professionals, and telecom enthusiasts.

Operating 100% through standard, documented Android public SDK APIs without requiring root privileges, kernel patches, or diagnostic daemons, RFeX Drive Lite delivers honest, real-time wireless diagnostics for 5G NR, 4G LTE, 3G WCDMA, and 2G GSM cellular networks.

KEY INSTRUMENTS & CAPABILITIES:

• REAL-TIME RF METRICS DASHBOARD
Observe primary serving cell and neighbor cell parameters in real-time, including:
- Reference Signal Received Power (RSRP)
- Reference Signal Received Quality (RSRQ)
- Signal to Interference plus Noise Ratio (SINR)
- Received Signal Strength Indicator (RSSI)
- Timing Advance (TA) and Channel Quality Indicator (CQI)
- Physical Cell ID (PCI), Tracking Area Code (TAC), Cell Identity (ECI/NCI)
- Downlink Frequency, ARFCN / EARFCN channel raster, and Bandwidth

• SERVING & NEIGHBOR CELL MONITORING
Track active carrier aggregation status and real-time intra-frequency / inter-frequency neighbor cells reported by the Android telephony radio interface layer. Sort neighbor cells dynamically by RSRP, PCI, or ARFCN.

• INTERACTIVE ENGINEERING MAP & BASEMAPS
Overlay live drive-test routes with color-coded RF quality markers (RSRP/RSRQ/SINR). Choose from 100% free, keyless basemaps:
- OpenStreetMap Standard: Crisp, worldwide street cartography.
- Humanitarian Roads: High-contrast cartography optimized for route tracking.
- Esri World Imagery: High-resolution top-down aerial satellite photography.
- OpenTopoMap: Topographic elevation contours and rural roads.
- Offline Engineering Grid: Minimalist coordinate canvas for disconnected environments.

• 3GPP SECTOR VISUALIZATION & CELL DATABASE
Load up to 100 local site records via CSV or use the one-tap "Load Sample Sites" feature to display realistic 3-sector antenna azimuth wedges, beamwidths, and live serving/neighbor line-of-sight spider links.

• INDOOR FLOORPLAN DRAFT SKETCH & WALK TEST
Conduct indoor wireless surveys in facilities where CAD or floorplan files are unavailable:
- Built-in Blueprint Sketch Studio: Draft walls, corridors, rooms, and doors on a precision blueprint grid.
- Quick Architectural Templates: Instant corridor and multi-room floorplans.
- Pinpoint Walk Test: Tap floorplan coordinates to drop geo-relative RF measurement points.
- On-Canvas RSRP Legend: Immediate visual KPI assessment.
- Reset & Clear: Undo individual pins or safely clear all survey points.

• DRIVE TEST RECORDING & CSV EXPORT
Record synchronized GPS coordinates and RF measurements in 5-minute outdoor drive-test sessions. Export clean CSV logs directly to device storage via Android Storage Access Framework (SAF) for post-processing in GIS or desktop RF planning tools.

• 100% ON-DEVICE PRIVACY GUARANTEE
Your telecommunications data and location belong to you. RFeX Drive Lite features:
- ZERO tracking beacons or analytics SDKs
- ZERO advertising libraries
- ZERO background data exfiltration
- 100% local processing and storage

Engineered with precision by RF enthusiasts at RFeX Technology for telecom professionals worldwide.
```
