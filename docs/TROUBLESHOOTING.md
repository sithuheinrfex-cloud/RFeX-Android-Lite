# Troubleshooting

- **Missing RF measurements:** Check the active SIM, device location settings, permission prompts and network registration.
- **No neighbors or 5G data:** Device firmware or operator service may not expose them.
- **No route position:** Check location services and outdoors GNSS availability.
- **Blank map:** Check connectivity and provider availability or switch to a grid.
- **Import/export failure:** Reopen the Android document picker and choose a supported file/destination.
- **Speed test unavailable:** Check connectivity and provider service status.
- **Recording stops:** Check Lite session limits, notification permissions and storage.

Contact support@rfex.app without sending sensitive subscriber or customer data.
