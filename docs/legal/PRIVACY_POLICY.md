# RFeX Drive Lite — Privacy Policy Draft

**App:** RFeX Drive Lite (`com.rfex.drive.lite`)  
**Publisher:** RFeX Technology  
**Updated:** 18 November 2026  
**Contact:** privacy@rfex.app

**Status: release candidate, not yet approved as a live Play Console policy.** Match to the signed build and publish to a working HTTPS URL before release.

RFeX Drive Lite uses cellular state, device details and location to present RF measurements and help users create outdoor/indoor field-survey observations. Survey sessions, imported site files and exports are intended for local storage and user control.

**External services:** Online maps send requested tile coordinates and connection data, including IP address, to the configured provider. Opening the optional hosted OpenSpeedTest widget contacts OpenSpeedTest; running a test transmits test traffic and public IP information to that provider. See https://openspeedtest.com/privacy-policy.

**SDK and permission disclosures:** The final production dependency inventory, actual WebView traffic, logging/advertising/consent SDKs, precise/background location requests, backup behavior and deletion flows must be confirmed before making categorical privacy assertions or submitting the Play Data safety form.

Users may adjust permissions in Android settings, choose whether to use online services, and manage local surveys/exports where supported. Third-party services retain their own privacy practices. Privacy questions: privacy@rfex.app.
