# Permissions and measurement limitations

On first launch, choose **Enable Precise Location**, then **Precise** and **While using the app**. Location permission helps Android make available serving and neighboring cells, PCI, TAC, Cell ID and GNSS for maps and recordings. Choose **Continue with Limited Features** to skip. Reopen the RF permission link or Android Settings to change your choice.

Use **Allow SIM access** separately for available subscription labels and selection. On Android 13 and later, Lite offers the notification permission when an outdoor recording begins. The location foreground service supports a user-started session. File import and export use Android's document picker.

Enable device Location Services for location-linked measurements. A GNSS fix can take time. Android and your phone determine whether each field is reported, including Timing Advance. NR SCS is unavailable. An Android 5G NSA display indication and an observed secondary NR cell are distinct observations.
