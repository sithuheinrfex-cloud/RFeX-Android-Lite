# Permissions

RFeX Drive Lite may require **location** for cell identity, positioning and tagged measurements; **phone state** for permitted SIM/network information; **notifications** for recording status when required; and the Android **document picker** for import/export.

The final manifest and tested Android behavior determine the permissions actually shipped. No claims about background location, foreground service types, advertising identifiers or absent restricted permissions should be published without inspecting the final artifact. Users should be given clear permission explanations.
